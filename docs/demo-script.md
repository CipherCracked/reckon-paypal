# Demo script — three minutes, five screens

Record in the sandbox with the three demo products. Each screen is one beat.

1. **Storefront** (`/`) — "Three purchases, three different commitments: paid once, paid every
   month, paid in instalments. Watch what happens before any of them is agreed to."

2. **Product page** (`/product/mattress-financed`) — read the merchant copy aloud as a shopper would
   meet it: "0% APR, 12 payments of $105.00, plus a $60.00 origination fee." Say plainly: this is
   where most checkout flows stop.

3. **Consent screen** (`/consent/<orderId>?slug=mattress-financed`) — the three figures:
   charged today $105.00, across the term $1,320.00, cost to leave **Not stated**. Point at "Not
   stated": the seller published no cancellation cost, so the page says so rather than showing
   $0.00.

4. **Recognition check** — press "No, show me the breakdown", then read the itemisation. Then press
   "Skip this check" on a second pass and show that the payment path is unchanged: it is an aid,
   never a gate.

5. **Payment** — press "Pay now" and show the success state, then say what the app never did: it
   never asked the shopper to work out a total, never advised them, and never stored a profile.

Close on the recurring product (`/product/coffee-subscription`) for two seconds: $12.00 today,
$144.00 across twelve months, $0.00 to cancel.
